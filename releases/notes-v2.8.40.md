## Chamber temperature on the status page
The chamber temperature always showed a dash. Newer Bambu printer firmware reports it in a different place than older firmware, and the controller only looked in the old place. It now reads both, and the same for the bed temperature.

## How to update
On 2.8.25 or newer: open the update page and tap **Install 2.8.40** in the **Update from GitHub** card.
By hand: use **BLLC_V2.8.40-lite.bin.ota** if you're not sure which layout you have.
