## Two editions
Every release now comes in two editions, and **Update from GitHub** picks the right one for your controller by itself:
- **BLLC_V…bin.ota (full):** for controllers on the larger storage layout. Room for all current and future features.
- **BLLC_V…-lite.bin.ota (lite):** for controllers on the original BLLED storage layout. Same features today, kept small enough to fit. Future features that need a lot of space may be full-edition only.

**Updating by hand?** If you're not sure which layout you have, use the **-lite** file: it works on every controller. If you choose the full file on a controller that's too small, the update page says so and nothing changes.

The status page shows "lite" after the version number if you're on the lite edition.

## How to update
On 2.8.15 or newer: open the update page and tap **Install 2.8.20** in the **Update from GitHub** card.
On anything older, including the original BLLED firmware: download a file below (the -lite one if unsure) and choose it on the update page.
