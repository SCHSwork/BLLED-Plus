## Red looks red again, not pink
Filament colors and the color preview could look washed out, like a pastel, because the white LEDs were mixed in. A red filament (a real one is more like 193, 46, 31) came out pinkish.

Now:
- Filament colors: the part shared by red, green and blue is taken out before the color is brightened, so a red spool shows as a deep red. Greys and pale colors look the same as before.
- Color preview and saved colors: white is only mixed in for genuinely pale colors, and it fades in smoothly, so there's no sudden jump between a deep color and a pastel one.
- Navy and other dark blues shift a little toward pure blue. That's intended.

## How to update
On 2.8.25 or newer: open the update page and tap **Install 2.8.52** in the **Update from GitHub** card.
By hand: use **BLLC_V2.8.52-lite.bin.ota** if you're not sure which layout you have.
