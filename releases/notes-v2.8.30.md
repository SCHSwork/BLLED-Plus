## Back to the home page after an update
When an update from the **Update from GitHub** card (or **Move to the larger layout**) finishes and the controller is running the new version, the page now goes back to the home page by itself, the same way it already did after uploading a file.

Includes everything from 2.8.29: crash reports in the Activity log, and the printer connection is closed properly before restarts.

## How to update
On 2.8.25 or newer: open the update page and tap **Install 2.8.30** in the **Update from GitHub** card.
By hand: use **BLLC_V2.8.30-lite.bin.ota** if you're not sure which layout you have.
