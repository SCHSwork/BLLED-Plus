## Fix things from your phone
The status page's **Controller** card has two new buttons:
- **Reconnect to printer**: drops the printer connection and connects again right away. Try this first if the printer shows as offline.
- **Restart controller**: tap twice to confirm. Takes about 15 seconds, and doesn't affect the printer or a running print.

## How to update
On 2.8.6 or newer: open the update page and tap **Install 2.8.8** in the **Update from GitHub** card, or let it install automatically if you've turned that on.
On older versions: download **BLLC_V2.8.8.bin.ota** below and upload it on the update page.
