## Prints that finish during quiet hours
If quiet hours dim the strip (or turn it off), a print that finished overnight used to lose its finished color before morning: its timer ran out while nobody could see it. Now the finished color is held until quiet hours end, then shows with a fresh timer. Opening the door during the night clears it, since you've seen the print. When quiet hours end, the strip also goes back to its normal state, even if the idle timer ran out overnight.

## Dismiss a printer error (full edition)
The error alert on the status page now has a **Dismiss** button. The lights stop showing that error, for example "clean the lidar" in the middle of a long print. If the printer clears the error and it comes back later, it shows again. To ignore a code for good, use the ignore list in the settings.

## How to update
On 2.8.25 or newer: open the update page and tap **Install 2.8.49** in the **Update from GitHub** card.
By hand: use **BLLC_V2.8.49-lite.bin.ota** if you're not sure which layout you have.
