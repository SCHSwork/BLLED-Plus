## The strip no longer turns on by itself while following the printer's light
With **Follow printer** on, the strip sometimes turned on by itself even though the printer's light was off. It happened during update checks, or when the printer reported some other change. When the printer's light went off, the strip turned off once, but the next time anything made it redraw, it fell back to the idle color. Now, while the printer is idle and its light is off, the strip stays off. The door work light, the camera light, errors and the finished-print color still show as before.

## How to update
On 2.8.25 or newer: open the update page and tap **Install 2.8.41** in the **Update from GitHub** card.
By hand: use **BLLC_V2.8.41-lite.bin.ota** if you're not sure which layout you have.
