## Activity log
The status page has a new **Activity** card showing what the controller did recently: when it connected to Wi-Fi and the printer, lost the printer, restarted and why, and how updates went. Newest is at the bottom, and **Copy** grabs it to send to someone. It's kept through restarts, but not if the power goes off.

## How to update
On 2.8.6 or newer: open the update page and tap **Install 2.8.10** in the **Update from GitHub** card (on 2.8.6–2.8.8 it may need a second try).
On older versions: download **BLLC_V2.8.10.bin.ota** below and upload it on the update page.
