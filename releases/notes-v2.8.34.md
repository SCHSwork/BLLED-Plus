## Closing the door no longer turns the lights off
With **Idle after** turned off, closing the printer door turned the strip off every time, even during a print. This was left over from the original firmware, and it also cut off the door work light instead of going back to normal. Now closing the door puts back whatever was showing before. If the lights were off when you opened the door, they still go back off.

## Also
- The camera light sends one light-on command when Bambu Handy reports the camera opening twice in the same second.

## How to update
On 2.8.25 or newer: open the update page and tap **Install 2.8.34** in the **Update from GitHub** card.
By hand: use **BLLC_V2.8.34-lite.bin.ota** if you're not sure which layout you have.
