## Silent mode
Some controllers make a high-pitched whine while the strip is dimmed. That comes from how the LEDs are dimmed: they're switched on and off 5,000 times a second, which is right in the range people hear. **Silent mode** (Settings → Color balance) switches them 25,000 times a second instead, above human hearing. It's off by default. Turn it on if you hear the whine.
